Getting there

Apprenticeship, 4 years (paid while learning). About $1,000 a year in school costs, with pay while you learn. Pay starts near $52,700 and rises with experience. Student loans are paid off around age 31.

Questions

How much does a plumber make?

About $62,000 a year at the middle of a career (U.S. median, Bureau of Labor Statistics), or about $30 an hour. Starting pay is lower, around $52,700.

How do you become a plumber?

Installing and repairing water, gas and drain lines and fixtures. Learned through a paid apprenticeship of about four years, then a license. In the model: Apprenticeship, 4 years (paid while learning). About $1,000 a year in school costs, with pay while you learn.

When could a plumber retire?

With the defaults on this page (saving 10% of take-home, a 4% match, married at 28, two kids, a home at 32), about age 55, and from 47 to 60 depending on pay and markets. Saving more moves it sooner.

How these numbers are made: the FIRE Mission Starting Out model in today's dollars. Pay is the U.S. median from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (2023-24), rounded; starting pay is about 70% of it, rising to about 125% after 20 years. Defaults: start at 18, live at home to 22 with the rent invested, roommates to 24, $1,450 rent, a paid-off car with $150 insurance, married at 28 to a spouse earning $50,000, two kids from 30, a $400,000 home at 32 once affordable, saving 10% of take-home with a 4% match, 5% returns after inflation, Social Security from 67. The range runs from 25th-percentile pay with 3% returns to 75th-percentile pay with 7%. A sketch, not a forecast, and not financial advice.
