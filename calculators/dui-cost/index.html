Where the numbers come from

All-in estimates for a first DUI commonly run from about $10,000 to $25,000: fines and court fees, a lawyer, towing and impound, alcohol education classes, license reinstatement and an ignition interlock device. Car insurance often rises by about $1,000 to $2,500 a year and stays higher for three to five years. Costs vary a lot by state; type your own.

What it doesn't count

Lost work from a suspended license, a lost job, injuries, damage to others, or the effect of a record on future pay. Any of these can cost far more than the numbers here.

Questions

How much does a DUI cost?

Commonly $10,000 to $25,000 all in for a first offense, plus several years of higher car insurance. It varies by state and by what happened.

How long does a DUI raise car insurance?

Usually three to five years, depending on the state and the insurer.
