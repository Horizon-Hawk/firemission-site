The point isn't the coffee

Nobody needs to give up everything. The numbers show the trade-off so it's a choice, not an accident: what a habit costs over decades, and why starting to invest early matters more than the amount.

How it's figured

The daily cost × 365 is invested at the end of each year and grows at the return you choose, in today's dollars (after inflation). 5% after inflation is a common long-run assumption for a stock-heavy portfolio, not a promise.

Questions

What is $5 a day worth over 40 years?

About $73,000 spent; invested at 5% a year after inflation, about $220,000.

How much does smoking cost a year?

A pack a day at about $9 is around $3,300 a year, before any health costs.
