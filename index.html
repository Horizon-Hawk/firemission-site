Spending Investments & FIRE Taxes

Most banks and cards have Download or Export on the transactions page. Pick CSV, or "Quicken / QFX" if offered. A year of each account gives the best picture.

For investment accounts, add the activity export and the monthly statement PDFs. The statements name the account and give its value.

How it counts
  • Money between your own accounts (card payments, transfers) is matched up and left out. Load every account you can so it can find both sides.
  • Card payments for a card you didn't load count as spending, in one line, until you add that card's statement.
  • Income is deposits it can name (paychecks, military retired pay, VA, Social Security, interest) plus anything you tell it. Deposits it can't name aren't counted until you answer.
  • Refunds reduce spending in the category they came from. Savings is money sent to investment accounts.
  • Nothing is uploaded or stored. Your answers are kept in this browser so you don't answer twice; use Save my answers to keep them for next month.
