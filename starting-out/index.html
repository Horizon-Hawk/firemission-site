Questions

How accurate is it?

It is a sketch, not a forecast: today's dollars, median pay and rough national costs, with a range shown from lower pay and 3% returns to higher pay and 7%. Real lives vary a lot.

Does it count Social Security?

Yes. It estimates Social Security from the path's own earnings and counts it from 67 when working out when you could stop working.

Can a parent and kid share a scenario?

Yes. Copy a share link or save a small file; it holds only the choices, never any statements.
